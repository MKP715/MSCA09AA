2021-03-14

The verbal presentation will be brief so I can report on gathering Area 09’s group conscience on Agenda Item feedback, and my full report will be posted on the Delegate’s Bulletin Board.

MSCA 09 Rocks!

  • I attended the following nine District meetings on indicated dates: #08 and #14 on 2/17; #04 and #10 on 3/3; #07 on 3/8; #17 on 3/9; and #01/03 and #05 on 3/11.
  • I met with the Electronic Media Subcommittee (EMSC) on 2/15; District 05 Concepts V, VI, VII, and VIII workshop on 2/20; and District Committee Member (DCM) School on 2/26.
  • I continue to work in person with Hispanic Districts, visiting #20 on 2/22, and with the six Hispanic Districts for a presentation on how to gather data on 2/28, where I hand-deliver the Agenda Item summary tables and record the presentation for posting and independent viewing.
  • Receive call from Hiro of his resignation as alternate delegate on 3/8. So, I instigate Zoom call on 3/9 where there is a unanimous decision by six Board members to elect Jose A as the new alternate delegate, to be considered for confirmation at this Area Service Committee.

Activities within the Pacific Region

  • On 2/16, Zoom with 13 of 15 Pacific Region delegates for general sharing, and on 2/28 for a discussion of Pacific Region AA Service Assembly (PRAASA) delegate’s round table, where we decide to keep it in the region. I also attend Area 05 Delegate’s Sharing Session on 3/13.
  • PRAASA, whoop, whoop, right! I completed registrations for five GSRs. With 2,115 people registered and 1,100+ attending at one time, I was glued (with sandpaper eyes) to the screen for three days. Congratulations on Romy S for representing Area 09 as our one panel presenter this year!
  • I believe that recordings of PRAASA will be available (if not already) from Smile God Loves You at www.sgly.com.

All Things Conference-Related
