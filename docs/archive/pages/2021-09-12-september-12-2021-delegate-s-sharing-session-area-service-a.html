MSCA 09 Rocks!

  • Following are the regularly-scheduled Area meeting I attended since our 8/8 Area Service Assembly: Area 09 Board meeting in-person in Riverside on 8/15; Districts #09 and #19 on 8/15; #08 on 8/18 (after day hike in Santa Barbara); #04 on 9/1; #12 on 9/2; #15 on 9/7; #05 on 9/10 (in person); and #1/3 on 9/10 (“Why have I stayed in General Service?”)

Later today, we will be voting for our nominee.

On 9/5, I attend the combined meeting of Accessibilities/Cooperation with the Elder Community/Technologies committees, where I share a link on an “Underserved Communities” workshop on 9/18, 10 am to 1 pm, at Meeting ID: 875 7812 4484, Passcode: area08ws: https://www.dropbox.com/s/6otu8j26yhpo4ea/WS_Underserved_AA_District_06-19%20%281%29.pdf?dl=0
